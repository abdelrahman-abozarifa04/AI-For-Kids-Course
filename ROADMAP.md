# Roadmap

What we want to build next. Each item is open for contributors; comment on the linked issue before starting.

## Near term
- **Classroom pilots:** teach both tracks with real groups and revise lessons from [classroom reports](https://github.com/abdelrahman-abozarifa04/ai-for-kids-course/issues/new?template=classroom-report.yml).
- **English edition:** full translation of the kids' lesson plans and the teens' term pages.
- **Printable worksheets** for the teens track (unplugged versions of the convolution and neuron activities).

## Next
- **Middle track (ages 10–14):** bridge the unplugged track and Python, using block-based tools and Teachable Machine.
- **Card editor:** a small in-browser tool so teachers can design their own decks and export print-ready PDFs.
- **More Egyptian-Arabic data:** a small, openly licensed, community-collected dataset of dialect sentences for the NLP notebooks.

## Principles
- Kids' track stays device-free.
- Every notebook runs on a free CPU in under a minute, with no downloads.
- Every solution is executed in CI.
